Test split by true label:

| True label | Items | Caught deterministically | Deferred to judge | Decided wrongly |

| right_doc_wrong_passage | 10 | 6 | 4 | 0 |

| unsupported_claim | 10 | 0 | 10 | 0 |

Read it as: the free layer catches most citations that point at the wrong passage or the wrong opinion and is right when it speaks; it never calls a citation supported and defers every supported and unsupported_claim item, because those differ by a reversed outcome or a swapped standard that word overlap cannot see. Those go back to the caller with the CrossSource judge prompt. For reference, the CrossSource harness reports that judge prompt with claude-sonnet-5-5 at citation_check accuracy 1.00 on a 30-item sanity sample (15 citation_check items), not a full run.
